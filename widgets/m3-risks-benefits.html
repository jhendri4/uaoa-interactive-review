Potential risks

  • Creating and amplifying harm: Generative AI can produce or manipulate text, images, audio, and video quickly and at scale.

Potential benefits

  • Detecting harmful content: AI can help identify potentially harmful content and patterns across large volumes of online activity.
  • Supporting moderation: Automated systems can flag content for review and help moderators prioritise potential risks.
  • Supporting users: AI-enabled tools can help filter unwanted content and connect people with reporting and safety features.
