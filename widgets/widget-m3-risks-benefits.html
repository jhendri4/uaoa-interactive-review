Potential Risks

  • Creating manipulated content: Generative AI can produce convincing fake images, audio, and videos, including deepfakes that falsely portray athletes saying or doing things that never happened.
  • Amplifying harassment: AI-powered tools can generate and distribute abusive messages rapidly and at scale, increasing the reach and impact of online harassment.
  • Spreading disinformation and impersonation: AI can be used to create misleading content or impersonate individuals, potentially damaging reputations and deceiving audiences.

Potential Benefits

  • Detecting harmful content: AI can help identify potentially abusive messages and detect patterns of harmful behaviour across large volumes of online activity.
  • Supporting moderation: Automated systems can flag and prioritise potentially harmful content for human review, helping moderators assess incidents and take appropriate action.
  • Supporting users: AI-enabled tools can help individuals filter unwanted content, manage their online experience, and access reporting and safety features.
