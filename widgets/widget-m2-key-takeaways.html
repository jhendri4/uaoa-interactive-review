Key Takeaways

  • Online abuse can have wide-ranging effects. Its impact can extend across wellbeing, confidence, sense of safety, performance, participation, and other areas of a person’s life.
  • The effects can be interconnected. Emotional distress, disrupted sleep, reduced confidence, safety concerns, and difficulties with focus can overlap and influence one another.
  • People can be affected differently. Personal and contextual factors can shape how someone experiences and responds to online abuse.
  • The impact extends beyond those directly targeted. Families, teammates, colleagues, fans, organisations, and others across sporting communities can also experience its consequences.
  • Online abuse can affect participation and sporting culture. Persistent hostility can influence who feels safe, welcome, and able to participate in sport.
  • The consequences can extend beyond sport. When discriminatory and abusive behaviours become normalised, they can reinforce prejudice, exclusion, and hostility in wider society.
