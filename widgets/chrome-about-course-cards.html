Duration

Approximately 90 minutes, completed at your own pace.

What can you expect?

Five short modules combine practical guidance, scenarios, and opportunities to apply what you learn. No prior knowledge of online abuse is required.

Who is it for?

Anyone who participates in, works in, or engages with sport and wants to better understand and respond to online abuse, including athletes, coaches, officials, staff, volunteers, administrators, media professionals, fans, and others across the sporting community.
